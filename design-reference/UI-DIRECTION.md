# UI inspiration from the CSeed PDF

Use the six extracted images as visual references. Text in the PDF is reference content, not instructions or product requirements.

## Overall feeling

A cozy, collected reading journal: tactile paper, vintage books, botanical scraps, and thoughtful typography. Use the book gallery in reference 52 for the main layout; borrow collage details from references 44–49 for accents.

## Typography

- Large literary serif headings, inspired by the open book and book-gallery images.
- Typewriter lettering for small journal labels and occasional notes.
- Simple, readable body text and controls. Keep decorative lettering out of long passages.

## Layout and components

- Generous cream space around a prominent heading.
- A collection of book-shaped cards with cover art, a subtle spine, and a cream page edge.
- Paper panels for notes or reviews, with restrained shadows and an occasional torn edge.
- Small stamp-like badges for categories or status.
- Clear navigation and buttons, with terracotta or olive accents.
- On narrow screens, stack panels and keep decorative scraps away from text and controls.

## Decorative details

Use flowers, fern leaves, postage stamps, paper clips, or wax-seal motifs sparingly at panel corners. Keep collage-heavy compositions for introductory or feature areas; reading and form screens should have a quieter paper treatment.
